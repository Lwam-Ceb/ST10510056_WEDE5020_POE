Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services.

Do you build custom PCs?

Yes. JingoTech provides custom-built computers based on the customer's requirements and budget.

Can I request a quotation?

Yes. Customers can submit an enquiry to request a quotation for products, upgrades or custom builds.

Do you repair laptops?

JingoTech provides laptop and computer repair and upgrade services.

Do you provide delivery?

JingoTech provides in-house delivery to customers as part of the company's service offering.

Can someone help me choose computer parts?

Yes. Customers with limited technical knowledge can contact JingoTech for assistance choosing suitable products and components.

Where is JingoTech based?

JingoTech Provider is currently based in Cape Town, South Africa.

Do you plan to expand outside Cape Town?

Yes. The long-term vision of JingoTech is to expand beyond the Western Cape and establish branches across South Africa.

How do I book a service?

Customers can use the JingoTech enquiry page to submit a service request.
