OUR HISTORY

From NeinvalliZone to JingoTech Provider

JingoTech Provider is a South African technology company based in Cape Town.

The business was formerly operated under the name NeinvalliZone, where it focused on computer hardware, repairs and custom-built computers.

In 2021, ownership was transferred to Jingo, leading to the rebranding of the business as JingoTech Provider.

The long-term goal is to expand beyond the Western Cape and establish branches across South Africa.

OUR MISSION

Making technology accessible.

To make quality technology affordable and accessible by providing reliable computer products, superb technical services and customer support.

OUR VISION

Growing across South Africa.

To become one of South Africa's most trusted independent technology retailers and service providers by delivering innovative solutions and expanding nationally.
