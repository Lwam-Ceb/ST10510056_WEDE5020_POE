OUR HISTORY

From a local technology store to a growing technology provider.

JingoTech Provider is a fictional technology organisation founded in Cape Town in 2005.

The organisation was later re-established in 2021 under new ownership and direction.

The business was handed down from an earlier technology store known as NeinvalliZone, which shared a similar goal of supplying computer components, pre-built systems and technical assistance.

JingoTech continues this foundation while focusing on making technology easier to access for its customers.

Our Mission

To provide reliable technology products, repairs and support while making the process simple and understandable for every customer.

Our Vision

To grow beyond Cape Town and make dependable technology and technical assistance accessible to more communities.

Growing beyond Cape Town

One of JingoTech's long-term goals is to expand its services beyond Cape Town. The purpose of this expansion is to make technology products, repairs and support more convenient for customers in other areas.
